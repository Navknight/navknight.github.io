About

I'm a full-stack software engineer. Most of my work is backend platform services and browser tooling: auth, data pipelines, Chromium and extension features, and the build machinery that keeps them shipping. Security came with the territory, so I design with it in mind. I'm open to new roles.

Zscaler (Feb 2026 – Jul 2026)

SquareX was acquired by Zscaler, and I carried the same platform and Zero Trust Browser work forward into Zscaler's larger enterprise customer base. I rebuilt the Chromium fork's patch-management pipeline so it could keep absorbing major upstream upgrades — it's now carried the browser through 7 major Chromium versions — and shipped 56+ custom Chrome APIs for device compliance and threat detection. I also doubled throughput on the browser extension's security engine with a sub-millisecond pattern-matching rewrite and cut per-tab memory use by 30%, and ported the full browser protection stack to Firefox on Android.

SquareX (Jun 2024 – Feb 2026)

I joined as a summer intern and stayed on as a software engineer, building backend and browser-protection platform features used by 80+ enterprise tenants and 40,000+ concurrent users. That included the platform's OIDC authentication layer, an enterprise data-leak-protection service for Office and PDF files (later ported to WASM for speed), and the Zero Trust Browser's UI and content-isolation logic in Chromium. I also co-created browser.security, an open-source framework simulating 30+ browser-security evasion techniques, shown at DEF CON and covered by Forbes.
