About / The longer version

Hi, I’m Abhinav.

I’m a full-stack engineer who ended up living inside a browser. For 2 years I worked on one built to keep company data on company machines, and I had my hands in most of its layers. Next I’d like to design systems like that from the first box.

Zscaler Feb 2026 – Jul 2026

Zscaler bought SquareX, and I came along with the furniture. I was on the founding team of the Zero Trust Browser, so mostly it meant more customers and a lot more Chromium.

  • Wrote 20+ native extension APIs in C++ and Objective-C++ that read a laptop’s health: antivirus, disk encryption, MDM enrollment, certificates. 17 of them landed in 11 days. Together they expose 50+ signals, so the browser can check a device without a separate agent.
  • Rewrote the page masking that hides card numbers and personal data on any site, Google Docs included. The old version re-indexed the whole page every 500 ms. The new one only touches what changed, and it does 2× the work with about 30% less memory per tab.
  • Wrote the first commit of our Android browser, a Firefox fork, and designed the build tooling 3 other engineers then built on. I moved the whole security extension from desktop to Android myself.
  • Built download-to-cloud. When policy says so, your download goes to the company’s Google Drive, OneDrive or Box, and the local copy never lands.

SquareX Jun 2024 – Feb 2026

I joined as a summer intern and kept showing up. The platform ran for 80+ companies and 40,000+ people at once, which made every bug feel personal.

  • Owned the Go identity provider that Entra ID and Okta call when you sign in. If you aren’t on the Zero Trust Browser, you don’t get in. Later I rewrote most of it, so tenant config now hot-reloads over Kafka with no restarts.
  • Made 631 commits to the React admin console over 18 months. The biggest piece was a deployment-script generator: pick 1 of 8 MDMs, an OS and a browser, and it writes the install script for you.
  • Added TOTP two-factor sign-in, and put the product docs (public until then) behind a login.
  • As an intern, co-built browser.security. I wrote 29 of its 46 ways to sneak a file past a web security gateway (split over WebRTC, hidden inside images, that sort of thing) and the demo site. SquareX took it to DEF CON 32, and Forbes covered it.

On the side

Apps, libraries and the odd research project. The ones with an interesting decision get a drawing on the home page, and the rest are on the projects page. I keep notes on every DSA problem I solve, and the lab has 2 small games about caches and latency.

Tools

Mostly C++, Go, TypeScript and Python, with Rust and CUDA when a side project asks for them. Kafka, Redis, Postgres, GCP and Docker at work. I know more about Chromium and Firefox internals than is strictly healthy.

School

B.Tech in Computer Science, IIT Tirupati, 2021–2025. I was also elected Students’ General Secretary, and we finished that year net-zero on a budget that had been cut 25%. Codeforces Expert and LeetCode Knight, when the contests go my way.
